Shorts Stats privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

What it reads

With read-only access granted through Google sign-in, Shorts Stats reads channel and video statistics (views, watch time, retention) from the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, for channels its owner controls.

How the data is used

The numbers are turned into a daily summary that is sent to the owner's private Discord server. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Sign-in tokens are stored privately by the owner and used only to fetch these statistics. The tool never uploads, edits or deletes anything on YouTube.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Shorts Stats' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions can go to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
